01 · The open floor

Exposed brick, tall white ceilings, spiral duct overhead — and, as of week two, a lounge to match: a proper sofa, swivel chairs, an ottoman, and the bean bags that were here first. Flip the switch to see the same room as the working office, hung as an art gallery, or set up for an event.

02 · Rooms to work

Glass doors keep the light moving. The conference room seats eight under a wall display, worktables gather beneath the wire-globe pendant, and the glass offices come online one desk delivery at a time.

03 · The blueprint

Ground floor at 590 Howard. A long brick shoebox that splits neatly in two: kitchen, conference room and the big open floor up top; studio, phone rooms and the corner office down by the front door.
