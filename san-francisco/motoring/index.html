Neighborhood analysis (SFTW 2025 data): SoMa — including the 2nd St / South Park corridor and East Cut — hosted ~40% of last year's events, roughly 2× FiDi (#2). The "SoMa near 2nd Street" hypothesis is confirmed: a16z sits at 2nd & Townsend, South Park is VC row, Anthropic is at 500 Howard. Watch Jackson Square/FiDi — the fastest-rising cluster for VC dinner formats.
Gone or not bookable — don't route inquiries here: Bespoke (Westfield) and Galvanize (44 Tehama) are closed; so are Blue Bottle Mint Plaza, Vega Coffee (Folsom St), and Bluestone Lane 685 Market. WeWork and Industrious SF locations have no public event-rental program, and South Park Commons is members-only — a partnership ask, not a rental.
Seed-venue corrections: Motoring Coffee is at 1525 Union St (Cow Hollow), not Russian Hill. SOHN is in Dogpatch (2535 3rd St). TIAT is at 151 Powell (Union Square), not SoMa. Bluestone Lane has no 101 California or Levi's Plaza café — its real downtown trio is 55 2nd St, 227 Front St, and 420 Folsom.
